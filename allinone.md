## Summary of all searched results

### 1. Overview

The API corpus contains 40 unique records from 2021-2026. It covers stock
incremental learning, finance-specific concept drift, drawdown optimization,
first-passage probability, and several unrelated uses of the same terms.

### 2. Trends

Concept-drift work moves from generic detectors in 2021 toward representation-
based real-time detection in 2025. Finance-specific results are much scarcer
than general streaming-learning papers. The phrase “first passage” is highly
ambiguous: many top results concern physics, structural safety, or percolation,
so title-level retrieval alone is not sufficient evidence for S20.
